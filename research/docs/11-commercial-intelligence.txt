# A report should separate observation from interpretation

Example:

### Observed

> 11 independent comments across four threads describe phone bounce or storage interference during longer runs.

### Interpretation

> Low-bounce storage therefore appears to be a meaningful physical job for this scope.

### Creative implication

> “Stop carrying your phone like this on long runs” may be a promising ad angle.

Those are **three different authority levels**.

Do not render them as one paragraph that sounds equally factual.

# Final Fable delta

I would give Fable this as an additional requirement:

> **Add a shared Commercial Intelligence reporting layer consumed by both `OPPORTUNITY_RESEARCH` and `NICHE_LOADOUT`. Do not duplicate market/style/ad analysis inside each mode. Each mode produces canonical evidence-backed run state; a deterministic intelligence compiler projects that state into mode-specific `ReportModel`s containing market research, style/aesthetic intelligence, product analysis, competitive/supplier analysis, and evidence-grounded market/product/collection/ad angles.**
>
> **Make `MarketAngle`, `ProductAngle`, `StyleAngle`, `CollectionAngle`, `AdAngle`, `AdCreativeBrief`, `StorefrontStrategy`, and `AnalysisChain` first-class Work Graph objects. θ may generate interpretations and creative angles, but φ must require evidence lineage, enforce claim authority, deduplicate equivalent angles, detect genericness, and ensure creative claims do not exceed the evidence. Clearly distinguish OBSERVED evidence, INFERRED analysis, SIMULATED hypotheses, CURRENT signals, and CREATIVE recommendations in both canonical JSON and HTML.**
>
> **For every important recommendation preserve the chain `evidence → observation → interpretation → market implication → product implication → creative implication`. Reports should answer not only what to sell, but why the market matters, which sub-niche/context matters, how current competitors/products behave, what product mechanism/delta matters, how the niche currently looks and talks, which positioning angles are defensible, which ad stories are strongest, and what evidence supports each conclusion.**
>
> **For `OPPORTUNITY_RESEARCH`, center the report on one opportunity and its market/product/positioning/ad strategy. For `NICHE_LOADOUT`, center it on community world, sub-niches, activity/lived-situation intelligence, style world, final 3–6-item collection, storefront thesis, collection positioning, and multiple TikTok/ad angles. Both should retain supplier/current-price/product-image/source information and evidence audit.**
>
> **Generate reports only downstream of canonical state. Report generation cannot create evidence, upgrade qualification, alter verdicts, or manufacture unsupported market claims. HTML is a view; SQLite/Work Graph remains canonical.**

That completes the business-facing side of the architecture. You aren't doing all this analysis merely to output **`QUALIFIED`** or **`LOADOUT_READY`**. The system should hand you a reusable **commercial intelligence packet**: what the market is doing, why the niche cares, what to sell, how to position it, what visual world fits it, what angles to test, how to structure the ad, what competitors/suppliers look like, and exactly which evidence supports each conclusion.
