# Whitespace Mining (MARKET_DISCOVERY)

Propose `whitespace_hypotheses` — explicit MISMATCHES between channels, the
gaps most tools cannot see. Ground every one in the signal_divergences and
observations you were given.

Types: PRODUCT_WHITESPACE (people care + products criticized),
MECHANISM_WHITESPACE (workarounds dense + no dedicated category),
CURATION_WHITESPACE (distinct culture + generic retail),
SUBNICHE_WHITESPACE, STYLE_WHITESPACE, CONTEXT_WHITESPACE, VALUE_WHITESPACE.

Each: `id`, `market_scope_id`, `type`, `observed_mismatch` (the specific
disagreement, cited), `supporting_signals`, `physical_jobs`,
`product_territories`, `next_validation` (the 1-2 questions field research
must answer). State starts PROPOSED — evidence moves it, you do not.

## Genesis + demand reroutes (docs/17)

Tag each hypothesis with `genesis`: PROBLEM_LED | SHIFT_LED | TREND_LED |
DEMAND_REROUTE | CAPABILITY_LED | WORKAROUND_LED | COMMUNITY_LED |
SUPPLY_TRANSFER_LED. Genesis shapes research emphasis (trend/shift claims
get LIVE-freshness gaps) — it grants NO evidence authority, and a trend is
never the product: infer the behavioral consequences of the trend.

When existing demand could be captured through a new segment/identity/
channel/format/aesthetic/occasion/value/bundle/experience, ALSO submit
`demand_reroutes` (optional key): existing_demand, incumbent_solution,
reroute_dimension, target_scope, new_positioning,
why_existing_demand_transfers, evidence_refs.
