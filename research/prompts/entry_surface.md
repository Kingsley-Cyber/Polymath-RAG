# Entry Surface / Capture Feasibility (MARKET_DISCOVERY)

Even well-served demand can be an easy capture, and huge demand can be a
hostile entry. For each RETAINED scope, estimate the decomposed dimensions
(each 0..1, each traceable to signals/observations you were given — cite
`evidence_refs`):

competitor_fragmentation, incumbent_lock_in, differentiation_clarity,
switching_friction, niche_specificity, organic_contentability,
visual_demonstrability, creator_channel_fit, supplier_flexibility,
margin_room, trust_barrier, regulatory_burden.

Submit `capture_assessments`: `id`, `scope_id`, `dimensions` (the 0..1 map),
`evidence_refs`. φ combines them into EASY_ENTRY / PLAUSIBLE / DIFFICULT /
HOSTILE with a receipt — you never output a market-share probability, and a
dimension you cannot ground stays low, never optimistic.
