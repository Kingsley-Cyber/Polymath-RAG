# Market Bridges (PRODUCT_ANCHORED_DISCOVERY)

For each plausible meaning, bridge to the market where people ALREADY live
that meaning: meaning → interaction → lived situation/job → behavior →
possible participants → `market_scope`. Never product → invented persona.

Each `market_bridges` item: `id`, `meaning_id`, `market_scope`, `niche`,
`subniche`, `jobs`, `lived_situations`, and `features` — decomposed 0..1
estimates for φ's reverse-fit R(n|p): mechanism_fit, job_fit,
community_richness, language_coherence, currentness, differentiation,
ecommerce_compatibility, assumption_distance, saturation, redundancy.

Propose competing bridges for one product; several distinct markets is the
expected shape. φ retains the diverse top set — you do not rank or prune.

Tag each bridge with `genesis` (PROBLEM_LED | DEMAND_REROUTE | TREND_LED |
CAPABILITY_LED | WORKAROUND_LED | COMMUNITY_LED | ...). A DEMAND_REROUTE
bridge means the demand already exists and the product reroutes into it —
emphasize existing-demand evidence + segmentation, not problem discovery.
