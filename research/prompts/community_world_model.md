# Community World Model (NICHE_LOADOUT)

Build the `world_model` — an accurate model of how this community actually
LIVES the activity. The product finder is downstream of this; a wrong world
model produces a plausible-looking loadout an insider would clock instantly.

Produce one object:

- `activities`: the real atomic activities inside the scope (mine BEFORE /
  TRANSITIONS / AFTER, not just the during-moment)
- `experience_profiles`: how beginners / intermediates / experienced people
  differ in behavior, gear judgment, and accepted suffering
- `constraints`: physical, social, budget, and rule constraints the scope
  imposes (these bound every later product slot)
- `norms_rituals`: recurring practices, meetup structures, etiquette
- `insider_language`: terms members actually use (flag which you OBSERVED in
  lane signals vs inferred — inferred terms are hypotheses)
- `moments`: the recurring lived moments a collection must serve
- `open_questions`: what you do NOT yet know about this world

Ground every element you can in frontier branches and registry priors given
to you; mark the rest as inferred. Do not propose products here — a world
model that already contains its conclusions is contaminated.
