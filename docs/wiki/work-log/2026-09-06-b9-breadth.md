# WORK LOG — B9 BREADTH-V1

## Contract

An explanatory question reaches the books that discuss the same principle in other words, through the same judged path as every other aspect; identifier lookups and plain factual questions are never widened.

## Changes

- `chat_plan.py`: `"ADJACENT"` in `QUERY_TYPES`; `ADJACENT_MAX = 1`; `ADJACENT_TASKS`; the prompt rule (what it is, when to emit it, weight 0.6, when to skip it); `validate_plan` drops ADJACENT for tasks outside the allow-list and demotes extras to MECHANISM.
- Test: `test_adjacent_aspect_is_a_known_type_capped_at_one_and_only_for_synthesis_tasks`.

## Proof

Live compiler, the same questions before and after, HYBRID, `presentation_probe.py` + receipts (`b9_analyze.py`). Frozen-plan fixtures are unchanged by construction (their plans are recorded), so the gate is what the live compiler now asks for and what that reaches.

**Factual questions (fixture B, first 10 — GROUNDED_QA, where ADJACENT must NOT appear):**

| metric | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| plans with an ADJACENT query | 0 / 10 | 0 / 10 (correct: never for plain QA) |
| queries per plan p50 | 1 | 1 |
| documents in union / judged / final (p50) | 11 / 11.5 / 6 | 14 / 14 / 6 |
| compiler wall p50 / fallbacks | 1.99 s / 0 | 2.06 s / 1 |
| citation precision, tagged (10-question LLM check) | — | 1.0, 10 / 10 (106 tags) |
| answer chars p50 / wall p50 | 2,276 (probe) | 2,065 (check) / 44 s |

**Synthesis-shaped questions (fixture M, first 10 — "Compare what the book says about X with Y", GROUNDED_SYNTHESIS):**

| metric | before | after, first wording | after, prescriptive wording |
|---|---|---|---|
| plans with an ADJACENT query | 0 / 9 | 0 / 8 | 6 / 9 |
| queries per plan p50 | 2 | 2.5 | 3 |
| documents in union / judged / final (p50) | 18 / 20 / 8 | 16.5 / 17 / 7.5 | 17 / 18 / 9 |
| distinct documents in the final sets (10 turns) | 36 | 33 | 43 |
| compiler wall p50 / fallbacks | 2.48 s / 0 | 2.29 s / 0 | 2.58 s / 0 |
| tags p50 / wall p50 | 10 / 46 s | 8.5 / 47 s | 15 / 44 s |

The first wording ("ADJACENT (at most one, only for …)") was permissive and the compiler model never used it — 0 of 18 plans across both runs. The one corrective attempt made the rule prescriptive ("for GROUNDED_SYNTHESIS or CREATE_FROM_KNOWLEDGE, ALWAYS include exactly ONE query of type ADJACENT") with a JSON example and a line in the disambiguation examples. With the prescriptive rule the compiler emitted an ADJACENT query on 6 of 9 synthesis plans (e.g. "how a body communicates force and intent to an observer" was the seed example; live: "how different representations of image or video data affect compatibility and display", "how visual and auditory elements coordinate to convey meaning in a scene"), plans went from 2 to 3 queries at the median, documents in the final set rose from 7.5 → 9 per turn and from 33 → 43 distinct across the ten turns, citation tags per answer from 8.5 → 15, and the compiler cost +0.3 s p50 with no fallback. The factual questions stayed untouched (0 / 10 ADJACENT, precision 1.0, 10 / 10 tagged). Gate MET; the residual is the 3 of 9 synthesis plans where the model still skipped the aspect.

## Rejected claims

- "A second embedding model for cross-domain matching" — not taken: the ADJACENT text is embedded and judged exactly like every other aspect; the change is in what the compiler asks for, not in the index.
- "Reserve extra seats for ADJACENT beyond an aspect's usual three" — not taken until measured; the aspect seats plus B11's document-fair prefix are the mechanism.

## Open contract gaps

- ADJACENT applies to synthesis and creation tasks only; a plain "what does the book say about X" stays one PRIMARY query, so the breadth the owner asked for arrives on explanatory questions, not lookups.
- The compiler model follows prescriptive rules and ignores permissive ones (0 / 18 plans under "at most one"); future aspect rules should be written as obligations with an example.
- The compiler model honours "ALWAYS include exactly ONE" on 6 of 9 synthesis plans, not 9; a deterministic post-fill (synthesising an ADJACENT query from the PRIMARY when the model omits it) would close the gap but would put the compiler's judgement in a template — recorded, not taken.
