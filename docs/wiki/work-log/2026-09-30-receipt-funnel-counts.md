# RECEIPT-FUNNEL-COUNTS: the dropped funnel keeps its counts

## Contract
- Found while confirming CI after CODE-WIKI-V1: `tests/determinism/test_chat_funnel.py::test_receipt_meta_is_never_sliced_into_invalid_json`
  started failing in CI at the push that carried RECEIPT-SHRINK-ORDER (`261635b6`, 2026-09-28) and was the ONLY test that push
  added to the red list (the F3/F5/F6 push: 12 failing; the F7 push: 13). The slice ran only `tests/contracts` locally.
- The test fills a receipt with a 3,000-row legend and expects the funnel's `counts` to survive; RECEIPT-SHRINK-ORDER drops the
  funnel FIRST (after compacting it), whole, so the counts went with it (`KeyError: 'counts'`).

## Changes
- `_meta_json`: when the drop order reaches the funnel, it becomes `{"truncated": "ids_dropped", "version", "counts",
  "lane_counts"}` instead of `{"truncated": True}`. The forensic core (legend, used evidence, plan) is still dropped last.

## Proof
- `tests/determinism/test_chat_funnel.py` + `tests/contracts/test_receipt_shrink_order.py`: 9 passed (the new contract test
  `test_a_dropped_funnel_keeps_its_counts` pins the counts and the dropped id lists).
- The code wiki page of `query_receipts.py` regenerated by `scripts/codewiki/pages.py refresh` (the maintenance loop).

## Rejected claims
- "The red CI is the wiki push's doing": the wiki push changed no failing test (1 contracts / 13 determinism before and after);
  the other 12 determinism failures and the contracts one (no `litellm` / `torch` in CI, fleet-DB adapter tests, the known
  compiler-route baseline) predate it.
