## Rejected claims

- "GRAPH as its own retrieval engine (graph-retrieval-v1 with `unassigned_rescue_evidence`)." Rejected by the plan (§3.15, §3.18, §3.21 #5–#6): graph is a bounded stage after evidence, seeded from the final candidates of every lane; v1 stays reachable only behind `retrieval: v1` / `latent`.
- "Embed the query again for the wildcard sweep (as the draft did)." Rejected: one embedding per distinct query text is the P1.d contract; the sweep starts from the shared vector at the `on_context` seam.
- "Raise the frontier deadline until the full frontier fits (8 s benchmarked)." Rejected by measurement: at `POLYMATH_CHAT_WILDCARD_DEADLINE_S=8` the finish still timed out on 30 / 30 turns (extension p50 8.0 s, 0 bridges) — under enrichment load eight sequential validations sit behind a single-request reranker at 1–3 s each; the answer is to return what was validated within the budget, not to wait longer.
- "Let bridges into the evidence ranking when they score well." Rejected (§3.19): bridges join synthesis as a separate lane and never enter evidence ranking; `bridges_in_evidence` is a receipted zero.

## Open contract gaps

1. **The frontier's cost is sequential reranker calls.** `divergent_finish` validates each candidate parent (≤ `candidate_parents` 8) with one cross-encoder call on that parent's children against its latent abstraction — the objective the plan keeps as is. At the ~240 ms/pair the P1.d judge measurements record, the full frontier would fit the +2.0 s budget; under enrichment contention one validation took 2.2 s p50 (finish p50 of the validated turns) and the frontier does not. The deadline-aware finish returns what it validated within the budget (`partial`, `parents_validated`, `parents_skipped` receipted); the numbers in Proof say how many bridges that yields at the 2.5 s default under today's load. A calm GPU (or the owner's §3.23 levers) is what restores the full frontier.
2. **An in-flight validation can overrun the deadline by one call.** The route waits a bounded 1.5 s grace for the partial result; beyond it the frontier is abandoned and any late call raises instead of touching the stores or the reranker. Before this fix an abandoned finish's in-flight reranker call bled into the next turn (back-to-back WILDCARD turns inflated each other in the first measurement).
3. **GRAPH has no deadline of its own.** Neo4j expansion is bounded by seeds and facts, not by wall-clock; a hung Neo4j would hold the turn as `graph.py` does today (measured graph p50 in Proof). A deadline like the frontier's is a one-line addition if the owner wants it.
4. **`graph_seeds_p50` is the offered-seed proxy** (surfaces + cards, capped); `_neo4j_expand` does not report the resolved seed count. `_selected_surfaces` iterates a set, so seed order is deterministic per process only.
5. **`/chat` GRAPH stays on graph-retrieval-v1** (its bundle shape differs); P1.f's runtime unification routes `/chat` through the stream's generator, which carries the v2 GRAPH composition.
6. **Frozen plans measure the modes on PRIMARY-only turns**; multi-aspect plans run their subqueries under every composition unchanged (the compositions differ only in lanes and the extra stage).
