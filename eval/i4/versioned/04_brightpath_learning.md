### Brightpath Learning: Platform Brief

Brightpath Learning uses the Mentor assessment engine for adaptive quizzes. The company is headquartered in Raleigh. Daniel Okafor works for Brightpath Learning as a curriculum designer.

The mentor engine depends on the QBank item database for item difficulty. Brightpath Learning acquired the Coachlight review app last autumn. The spaced repetition schedule influences retention across cohorts.

The company considered replacing the Mentor engine but kept the existing setup. Two rival providers use the same assessment format. The company hired three new instructors last month. Brightpath has not adopted the Coachlight app for high school courses.
