### Corval Logistics: Regional Operations Review

Corval Logistics uses the FreightNet routing platform for regional hauls. Corval Freight is a subsidiary of Corval Logistics. The Portside warehouse is located in Oakland.

Corval linked the QuickScale invoicing system to the FreightNet routing platform in June. The regional dispatchers joined the West Coast logistics consortium.

Corval considered replacing FreightNet with a rival platform but kept the existing setup. The invoicing system was not responsible for the billing delays. The new pricing model has not been deployed. The harbor terminal was the actual source of the delays.
