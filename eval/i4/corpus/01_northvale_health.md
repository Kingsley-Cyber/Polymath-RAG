### Northvale Health Network: Operations Review

Northvale Health Network uses the CareChart EMR platform across its three hospitals. Lakeshore General Hospital is located in Austin, and its nursing stations run the platform around the clock. Dr. Amara Osei serves as chief medical officer of Northvale Health Network.

The CareConnect portal depends on the CareChart EMR for scheduling data. The radiology review board is part of Lakeshore General Hospital and meets every Tuesday. Dr. Amara Osei founded the Riverside Clinic in 2015 before joining the network.

The hospital evaluated the CareCoordinator scheduling model but has not adopted it. The EMR outage did not affect the patient portal. The hospital transfers patient records to the state registry after discharge, and the team uses the portal during handoffs. The company hired two new surgeons last month.
