### Crestline Automation: Line Efficiency Review

Crestline Automation uses Siemens PLCs on the assembly line. The Crestline plant is based in Toledo and runs two shifts. Maria Kowalski leads the Crestline automation team.

The analytics team created the shift scheduling model last year. The scheduling model enables same-day dispatch for urgent orders. Crestline developed the Cobalt assembly cell with the robotics vendor.

Crestline linked the vision system to the quality database in March. The pump failure caused the production stoppage on line two. The vision system was not responsible for the defect spike. The team did not deploy the Cobalt cell on line one. The plant evaluated a night-shift pilot but has not adopted it.
