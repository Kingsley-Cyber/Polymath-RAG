# Why Your Shopify Conversion Rate Can Look Better While Your Store Gets Worse

If you look at conversion rate alone, your store can seem healthy while revenue falls. A lower-price product line lifts the conversion metric without improving contribution margin. Buy-one-get-one bundles spike checkout completions and compress average order value.

The visitor mix matters more than the headline number. Paid ads bring cold traffic that converts worse than email subscribers. When the ad budget grows, conversion rate drops even though the store is earning more.

Cart abandonment is another misleading signal. Session replay shows users abandoning at the shipping-cost step, not the product page. Fixing the funnel requires segmenting first-time visitors from returning customers and measuring customer acquisition cost per channel.

The metric that actually tracks health is net revenue per visitor, because it combines conversion rate with average order value. A store that trades margin for conversion is getting worse while its dashboard looks better.
