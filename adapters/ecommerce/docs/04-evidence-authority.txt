Yes. Keep Fable’s current implementation exactly where it is. This is an **integration delta** that strengthens the physical-product graph and loops by making every evidence item answer two separate questions:

# 34. Final Control Law

Give Fable this verbatim:

> **Relevance determines whether evidence is worth considering. Evidentiary authority determines what that evidence is allowed to establish. These are never interchangeable. Every admitted evidence item must declare its KnowledgeRole, EvidenceRole, authority, freshness, source identity, and establishment limits. Every hypothesis, physical-product candidate, and supplier candidate must be evaluated against explicit required evidence roles rather than raw evidence counts.**

And:

> **Physical products may emerge from Polymath reasoning, comments, reviews, workarounds, Amazon, CJDropshipping, Alibaba, or other approved web research. Discovery origin does not grant evidentiary authority. A supplier listing proves supply, not demand. A comment can demonstrate a complaint or workaround, not population prevalence. An evergreen book can support a mechanism, not a current market state.**

And:

> **When required evidence roles remain unsatisfied, the loop must generate the next query against a source capable of satisfying those roles. Every query must declare why that source was selected, which evidence roles it seeks, and which requirements that source cannot satisfy.**

And finally:

> **The registry remains a reasoning seed rather than a closed world. EXACT, INFERRED, MULTIPLE, and UNRESOLVED resolution are all valid. An unresolved niche or lens must not force a nearest-neighbor classification; Hermes may investigate the open-world behavior directly and create provisional niche, market-identifier, or perspective candidates without blocking physical-product discovery.**

That completes the physical-product graph/loop design without changing Fable's current architecture.

The resulting system is not merely:

```text
find enough evidence
```

It becomes:

```text
find the RIGHT kind of evidence
from a source qualified to provide it
at the freshness required by the claim
until every necessary part of the
physical-product opportunity graph is satisfied.
```

That is the control that makes a corpus containing almost anything useful rather than dangerous.
