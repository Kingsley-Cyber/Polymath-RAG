U(b∣s)=αΔJ+βΔF+γΔP+δI+ϵX+ζC−λR−μD−νK

The deeper objective

Your competitive advantage should not be:

“We have products for hikers.”

It should be:

“We have mapped the little situations that only become obvious after you have actually done this activity repeatedly.”

Those little situations are where products come from:

the thing you always have to stop to retrieve

the layer everyone quietly replaces

the equipment everyone modifies

the bag that works until you bring children

the clothing cut experienced runners migrate toward

the item nobody buys until their first long-distance attempt

the workaround soldiers use because the issued solution technically works but practically sucks

the object that becomes annoying only after two hours

the thing beginners overpack

the thing experienced participants refuse to carry

That is the real insider-commerce signal.

So I would make CommunityWorldModel → LivedSituations → Experience-conditioned field correction → Insider Fidelity the centerpiece of the mode.

The product finder is downstream of that.

If you nail that layer, the storefront should feel less like “someone researched hiking products” and more like “someone who hikes built this collection.”
