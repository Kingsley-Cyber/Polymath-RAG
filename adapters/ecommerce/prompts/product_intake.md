# Product Intake (PRODUCT_ANCHORED_DISCOVERY)

Capture the product seed EXACTLY as given, separated by authority:

- `user_name`: what the user calls it
- `description`: observable physical description only
- `user_hypotheses`: the user's market/use guesses — HYPOTHESES
- `seller_claims`: supplier/listing claims — CLAIMS, quarantined next step
- `intended_marketplace`, `price_point`, `source`: if given

Do not improve, endorse, or reframe any claim here. Health, cultural,
authenticity, rarity and origin claims especially must pass through
quarantine before they may ever appear as market or ad claims.
