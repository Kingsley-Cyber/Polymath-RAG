# Market Lattice (MARKET_DISCOVERY)

Propose `market_scopes` — lattice cells, NOT a taxonomy tree. A scope is a
market crossed with any useful axes (activity, experience level, social
setting, life intersection, context): "runner × high-mileage × hot-weather".
Interesting markets usually live at intersections.

Each scope: `id`, `market`, `niche`, `subniche`, `dimensions`, `origin`
(which lane surfaced it — FIELD | TRENDS | CORPUS | SUPPLY | REGISTRY |
THETA), and `features` — decomposed 0..1 estimates with lineage to lane
signals: attention, community, whitespace, job_richness, currentness,
sourceability, transfer, redundancy, saturation. φ combines these into M(s);
you never rank scopes yourself. Estimate honestly: features you cannot trace
to a signal stay low. Include scopes from EVERY lane, not just the loudest.
